From pilot to rollout

Read the story. Tap the underlined words to see what they mean in English.

A bank wanted to make it easier for new customers to open an account. First, the team tested a simpler onboarding process with a small group. The pilot worked well, so the bank decided to roll out the new process in its mobile app. Then, using customer segmentation, the team saw which groups of customers used the app the most. Because the results were so good, the bank decided to scale up the initiative: it offered the simpler process to more customer groups and added it to other digital channels, such as its website.
